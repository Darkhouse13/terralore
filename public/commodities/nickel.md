# Nickel — who supplies the world

World nickel mine production: 3.9 Mt in the 2025 USGS estimate. Indonesia leads with 67%; the top three hold 79%. Every producer's sourced share.

Canonical: https://terralore.co/commodities/nickel
Updated: 2026-08-11 (USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries edition)

What is measured: the MCS statistics row "Mine production" — mine production in tonnes, what left mines in each calendar year. Not reserves in the ground, and not refined or smelted output. 2025 is the USGS estimate; 2024 is the reported figure.

World total: 3.9 Mt (2025 est.) · 3.7 Mt (2024).
Shares are computed against the published world total itself, never against the sum of listed producers.
