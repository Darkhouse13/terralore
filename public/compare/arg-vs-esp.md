## Entangled histories (4 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 1516 CE: **Spanish reach the Rio de la Plata** — European expeditions begin to explore the great estuary of the Rio de la Plata, opening the region to Spanish ambitions. (from the Argentina chronicle; sources: Argentina)
- 1609-1767: **Jesuit missions among the Guarani** — The Jesuit Order founds mission towns among the Guarani that flourish until the order's expulsion from Spanish lands in 1767. (from the Argentina chronicle; sources: Jesuit Missions of the Guaranis; Argentina)
- 1778 CE: **Free trade reforms** — Bourbon commercial reforms loosen Spain's trade monopoly, allowing Buenos Aires to trade more freely and accelerating its growth as an Atlantic port. (from the Argentina chronicle; sources: Viceroyalty of the Rio de la Plata)
- 9 July 1816: **Declaration of Independence at Tucuman** — The Congress of Tucuman declares independence from Spain as the United Provinces of South America, signed by twenty-nine delegates under the presidency of Laprida. (from the Argentina chronicle; sources: Congress of Tucuman; Argentine Declaration of Independence)
