## Entangled histories (2 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 1912-13: **Conference of London and the borders** — The Great Powers recognised an autonomous Albania but, pressed by its neighbours, drew borders leaving about half the Albanian-populated lands, including Kosovo, outside the state, creating the Albanian national question. (from the Albania chronicle; sources: Treaties of London; Ismail Qemali Leads Albania to Independence, November 1912; History of Albania)
- 17 Feb 2008: **Kosovo declares independence** — Kosovo, whose population is overwhelmingly ethnic Albanian and which was left outside Albania in 1913, declared independence on 17 February 2008, recognised the next day by the United States. (from the Albania chronicle; sources: A Guide to the United States' History of Recognition, Diplomatic, and Consular Relations: Kosovo; Kosovo)
