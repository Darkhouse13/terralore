## Entangled histories (2 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- c. 9th century: **Rise of the Kanem empire** — Around Lake Chad the empire of Kanem emerged under the Sayfawa dynasty, growing wealthy as the southern terminus of the trans-Saharan trade and gradually adopting Islam. (from the Nigeria chronicle; sources: The Bornu Empire; Nigeria (country overview and history))
- c. 1400: **Shift from Kanem to Bornu** — After conflict with the Bulala forced the dynasty to abandon its eastern heartland, the state re-centred west of Lake Chad at Bornu, beginning the era of the Bornu Empire. (from the Nigeria chronicle; sources: The Bornu Empire; Nigeria (country overview and history))
