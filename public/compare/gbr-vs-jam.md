## Entangled histories (3 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 1655 CE: **English conquest** — An English force under Penn and Venables seizes the island for Cromwell, launching the sugar-and-slavery plantation economy. (from the Jamaica chronicle; sources: Jamaica: History; Jamaica: British rule)
- 1944 CE: **Universal adult suffrage** — A new constitution grants every adult the vote, the first in the British Caribbean, and Jamaica holds its first elections under it. (from the Jamaica chronicle; sources: Jamaica: The independent country)
- 1958 CE: **West Indies Federation** — Jamaica joins the federation of British Caribbean colonies as its largest member, an experiment in regional union. (from the Jamaica chronicle; sources: Jamaica: The independent country; The Federal Archives Fonds, 1958-1962 (West Indies Federation))
