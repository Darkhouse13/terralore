## Entangled histories (3 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 1862: **First treaty of friendship with Italy** — After Italian unification, San Marino and the new Kingdom of Italy concluded a treaty of friendship in 1862 confirming the republic's independence, the first of the agreements that secure its sovereignty as an enclave. (from the San Marino chronicle; sources: San Marino (Napoleon, Congress of Vienna, 1862 treaty); History of San Marino)
- 1862 onward: **Treaties of friendship with Italy** — San Marino's sovereignty as an enclave is secured by treaties of friendship and good-neighbourliness with Italy first concluded in 1862 and renewed in 1939 and later, regulating relations while guaranteeing independence. (from the San Marino chronicle; sources: San Marino (Napoleon, Congress of Vienna, 1862 treaty); History of San Marino)
- 1923: **Fascist seizure of power** — Amid postwar economic distress the Sammarinese Fascist Party took control in 1923 and dissolved the Grand and General Council, ruling the republic for two decades in step with Mussolini's Italy. (from the San Marino chronicle; sources: World wars and fascism in San Marino, 1922-1945; Anniversary of the Fall of Fascism and Freedom Day)
