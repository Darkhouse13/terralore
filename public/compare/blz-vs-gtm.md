## Entangled histories (4 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 1859: **The Anglo-Guatemalan boundary treaty** — Britain and Guatemala signed a treaty fixing the settlement's limits from the Río Hondo to the Sarstoon; the unfulfilled Article 7, promising a means of communication never built, became the root of Guatemala's later claim. (from the Belize chronicle; sources: The Belize–Guatemala Territorial Differendum (Peace Fund); Guatemala's Territorial, Insular and Maritime Claim (Guatemala/Belize), Case No. 177; Our History)
- 21 September 1981: **Independence as Belize** — Belize became an independent state within the Commonwealth on 21 September 1981 with George Price as first prime minister — the last British colony on the Central American mainland to win independence, its frontier still claimed by Guatemala. (from the Belize chronicle; sources: A Guide to the History of Recognition, Diplomatic, and Consular Relations, by Country: Belize; History of Belize; Our History)
- 8 December 2008: **Special Agreement on the dispute** — Belize and Guatemala signed a Special Agreement committing to submit Guatemala's territorial, insular and maritime claim to the International Court of Justice, subject to approval by referendum in each country. (from the Belize chronicle; sources: The Belize–Guatemala Territorial Differendum (Peace Fund); Guatemala's Territorial, Insular and Maritime Claim (Guatemala/Belize), Case No. 177)
- 2020 onward: **Pleadings before the World Court** — Guatemala and Belize filed written pleadings before the International Court of Justice in the case "Guatemala's Territorial, Insular and Maritime Claim (Guatemala/Belize)", moving toward a final, binding judgment on the century-old dispute. (from the Belize chronicle; sources: Guatemala's Territorial, Insular and Maritime Claim (Guatemala/Belize), Case No. 177)
