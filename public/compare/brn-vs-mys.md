## Entangled histories (3 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 1841: **James Brooke becomes Raja of Sarawak** — The Sultan of Brunei appoints the Englishman James Brooke raja of Sarawak, founding the 'White Raja' dynasty and beginning the absorption of the Borneo territories into the British sphere. (from the Malaysia chronicle; sources: Malaysia: Malaya and northern Borneo under British control)
- 8 December 1962: **The Brunei revolt** — The Parti Rakyat Brunei's armed wing launches an uprising against the sultanate and the Malaysia plan; British forces crush it within days, and the party is banned. (from the Brunei chronicle; sources: History of Brunei)
- 1963: **Brunei stays out of Malaysia** — Sultan Omar Ali Saifuddien III decides not to join the new Federation of Malaysia, keeping Brunei on a separate course toward eventual independence. (from the Brunei chronicle; sources: History of Brunei)
