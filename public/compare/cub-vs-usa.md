## Entangled histories (4 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 15 Feb 1898: **Sinking of the USS Maine** — The U.S. battleship Maine explodes in Havana harbour, propelling the United States toward war with Spain over Cuba. (from the Cuba chronicle; sources: The Spanish-American War, 1898)
- 10 Dec 1898: **Spanish-American War ends Spanish rule** — After Spain's defeat, the Treaty of Paris ends Spanish dominion over Cuba; the Teller Amendment had disclaimed U.S. annexation. (from the Cuba chronicle; sources: The Spanish-American War, 1898; Platt Amendment (1903))
- 20 May 1902: **Republic of Cuba proclaimed** — The United States ends its occupation and the Republic of Cuba is installed under Tomas Estrada Palma, its first president. (from the Cuba chronicle; sources: A Guide to the United States' History of Recognition, Diplomatic, and Consular Relations, by Country: Cuba; Tomas Estrada Palma)
- 17 Dec 2014: **Thaw with the United States** — Obama and Raul Castro announce normalisation; diplomatic relations are restored and embassies reopen in 2015, though the embargo remains. (from the Cuba chronicle; sources: Re-Establishment of Diplomatic Relations With Cuba; Cuba since 1991)
