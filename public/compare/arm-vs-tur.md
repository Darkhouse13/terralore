## Entangled histories (4 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 1828: **Russia annexes eastern Armenia** — Following war with Persia, Russia annexed the eastern (Persian) Armenian lands, dividing the Armenian homeland between the Russian and Ottoman empires. (from the Armenia chronicle; sources: Armenia — Caucasus, Soviet Union, Genocide (Modern Armenia))
- 1915–1916: **The Armenian Genocide** — From 24 April 1915 the Ottoman Young Turk government deported and massacred the empire's Armenian population in death marches toward the Syrian desert; widely recognised as the first genocide of the 20th century, with deaths estimated from roughly 600,000 to as many as 1.5 million. (from the Armenia chronicle; sources: The Armenian Genocide (1915–16): In Depth; Armenian Genocide)
- 2 December 1920: **Sovietisation of Armenia** — Squeezed between Turkish nationalist forces and the advancing Red Army, the First Republic fell and Armenia was Sovietised, ending its short-lived independence. (from the Armenia chronicle; sources: Armenia — Caucasus, Soviet Union, Genocide (Modern Armenia))
- 10 August 1920: **Treaty of Sèvres** — The Allied treaty with the defeated Ottoman government proposed partitioning Anatolia among Greece, Armenia, Italy, France and others; rejected by the nationalists, it triggered the War of Independence. (from the Türkiye chronicle; sources: Kemal Atatürk; Ottoman Empire)
