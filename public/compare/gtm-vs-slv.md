## Entangled histories (3 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 1524: **Alvarado's conquest begins** — An expedition from Guatemala under Pedro de Alvarado enters the region, meeting fierce Pipil resistance west of the Lempa River as the Spanish conquest of the territory begins. (from the El Salvador chronicle; sources: History of El Salvador; El Salvador: The colonial period)
- 15 September 1821: **Independence from Spain declared** — The Act of Independence of Central America, signed in Guatemala City, frees the region including the province of San Salvador from Spanish rule. (from the El Salvador chronicle; sources: Acta de Independencia de Centro America; Central American Federation; Central America: Independence, 1808-23)
- 1824: **Federal Republic of Central America** — The provinces adopt the constitution of the Federal Republic of Central America, a union of Guatemala, El Salvador, Honduras, Nicaragua and Costa Rica in which the Salvadoran Francisco Morazan becomes a leading figure. (from the El Salvador chronicle; sources: United Provinces of Central America; Central American Federation)
