- c. 1710: **Seku Watara founds the Empire of Kong** — Seku Watara united the Dyula of Kong into a state, often called the Wattara Empire, that grew rich controlling the gold and kola trade and reached into present-day Burkina Faso, Mali, and Ghana. (from the Ivory Coast chronicle; sources: Kong Empire; History of Ivory Coast)
