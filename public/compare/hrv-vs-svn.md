## Entangled histories (3 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 4 Nov 2009: **Arbitration Agreement with Croatia** — Slovenia and Croatia signed an Arbitration Agreement submitting their land and maritime border dispute, including Piran Bay, to the Permanent Court of Arbitration in The Hague. (from the Slovenia chronicle; sources: Arbitration (the border dispute with Croatia); Arbitration between the Republic of Croatia and the Republic of Slovenia (PCA Case No. 2012-04, Final Award 29 June 2017))
- 29 Jun 2017: **PCA award on the Piran Bay border** — The Permanent Court of Arbitration rendered its Final Award, fixing the land and maritime boundary through Piran Bay and granting Slovenia a junction to the high seas; Slovenia accepted the award, Croatia rejected it. (from the Slovenia chronicle; sources: Arbitration between the Republic of Croatia and the Republic of Slovenia (PCA Case No. 2012-04, Final Award 29 June 2017); Arbitration (the border dispute with Croatia))
- 29 Jun 2017: **Piran Bay arbitration award** — The Permanent Court of Arbitration issued its Final Award on the Croatia-Slovenia border in the Gulf of Piran; Croatia, which had withdrawn from the process in 2015, declared it did not consider itself bound by the ruling. (from the Croatia chronicle; sources: Arbitration (Croatia-Slovenia border, Final Award 29 June 2017))
