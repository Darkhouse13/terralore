## Entangled histories (4 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- later 18th century: **The Lao kingdoms become Siamese vassals** — Divided and weakened, the rulers of Luang Prabang, Vientiane, and Champasak each fall under a Siamese commissioner and must pay tribute to Bangkok. (from the Laos chronicle; sources: Lan Xang; Laos: History)
- 1828 CE: **Siam destroys Vientiane** — After King Anouvong's failed revolt against Bangkok, Siamese forces sack and raze Vientiane and deport its people, ending the kingdom of Vientiane. (from the Laos chronicle; sources: Vientiane; Laos: History)
- 1893: **Franco-Siamese crisis and loss of Laos** — After French gunboats threaten Bangkok, Siam cedes the Lao territories east of the Mekong to French Indochina, the heaviest of its frontier concessions. (from the Thailand chronicle; sources: Thailand)
- 1893 CE: **French protectorate established** — Following the Franco-Siamese crisis, Siam cedes the territory east of the Mekong and France makes the Lao states a protectorate within French Indochina, administered from Vientiane. (from the Laos chronicle; sources: Auguste Pavie; Laos: Under foreign rule)
