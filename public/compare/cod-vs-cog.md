## Entangled histories (2 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 1884–1885: **Berlin Conference divides the two Congos** — The Berlin Conference recognised French claims north of the Congo River and assigned the south bank to Leopold II's Congo Free State, fixing the lasting division between the Republic of the Congo and the future Democratic Republic. (from the Congo chronicle; sources: Republic of the Congo; History of the Republic of the Congo)
- May 1997: **Mobutu falls; Kabila takes power** — Mobutu flees into exile and dies months later; Kabila seizes Kinshasa and restores the name Democratic Republic of the Congo. (from the DR Congo chronicle; sources: Kabila, Laurent-Désiré (1939–2001); Mobutu Sese Seko, president of Zaire; Mobutu Sese Seko)
