## Entangled histories (3 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- c. 247 BCE: **Rise of the Parthian Empire** — The nomadic Parni founded the Parthian state, which grew into an empire ruling Iran and Central Asia for nearly five centuries and rivalling Rome, with Nisa among its early royal centres. (from the Turkmenistan chronicle; sources: Parthia; Parthian Empire)
- 2nd century BCE: **Royal city of Nisa** — Old and New Nisa, near modern Ashgabat, served as an early capital and royal residence of the Parthian Empire, with palaces, temples and treasuries that fused Hellenistic and Iranian art. (from the Turkmenistan chronicle; sources: Parthian Fortresses of Nisa; Parthia)
- c. 651 CE: **Merv under the Arab caliphate** — After the fall of Sasanian Persia, Merv became a key garrison and administrative centre of the Arab caliphate in Khorasan, a base for the spread of Islam and for campaigns further into Central Asia. (from the Turkmenistan chronicle; sources: State Historical and Cultural Park 'Ancient Merv'; Turkmenistan)
