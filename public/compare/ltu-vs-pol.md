## Entangled histories (7 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 1385: **Union of Krewo** — Grand Duke Jogaila agreed to marry Queen Jadwiga of Poland, convert to Christianity and unite the two realms; baptised as Wladyslaw II Jagiello, he founded the Jagiellonian dynasty. (from the Lithuania chronicle; sources: Grand Duchy of Lithuania; Union of Lublin)
- 1385: **Union of Krewo and the Jagiellonian dynasty** — The agreement at Krewo arranged the marriage of Queen Jadwiga to Jogaila of Lithuania, who was baptised as Wladyslaw II Jagiello, joining Poland and Lithuania under a new dynasty. (from the Poland chronicle; sources: Poland: History; Union of Lublin)
- 1387: **Christianization of Lithuania** — A bishopric was founded at Vilnius and Lithuania, the last major pagan state in Europe, formally accepted Latin Christianity following the union with Poland. (from the Lithuania chronicle; sources: Grand Duchy of Lithuania)
- 15 Jul 1410: **Battle of Grunwald (Zalgiris)** — Polish and Lithuanian forces under Wladyslaw II Jagiello and Grand Duke Vytautas annihilated the army of the Teutonic Knights, ending the Order's expansion in the Baltic. (from the Lithuania chronicle; sources: Battle of Grunwald (1410); Vytautas the Great)
- 1 Jul 1569: **Union of Lublin** — Poland and the Grand Duchy of Lithuania united into the federal Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth, ruled by an elected monarch and a common Sejm, with Lithuania keeping its own laws and institutions. (from the Lithuania chronicle; sources: Union of Lublin; History of Lithuania)
- 1 Jul 1569: **Union of Lublin** — Poland and the Grand Duchy of Lithuania united into a single federal state, the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth, ruled by an elected monarch and a common Sejm, one of Europe's largest powers. (from the Poland chronicle; sources: Union of Lublin; The Act of the Union of Lublin document)
- Oct 1920: **Zeligowski's Mutiny and the seizure of Vilnius** — Polish forces under General Zeligowski, secretly directed by Pilsudski, seized Vilnius and its region; a 'Republic of Central Lithuania' was created and annexed by Poland in 1922. (from the Lithuania chronicle; sources: Vilnius dispute)
