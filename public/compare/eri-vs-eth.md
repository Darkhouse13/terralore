## Entangled histories (8 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- October 1896: **Treaty of Addis Ababa** — Italy recognises the full sovereignty and independence of Ethiopia, abrogating the Treaty of Wuchale, though it retains the separate coastal colony of Eritrea. (from the Ethiopia chronicle; sources: Battle of Adwa)
- October 1935 - May 1936: **Conquest of Ethiopia launched from Eritrea** — Italian forces invade Ethiopia from Eritrea, taking Addis Ababa in May 1936; Eritrea is absorbed into Italian East Africa. (from the Eritrea chronicle; sources: Second Italo-Ethiopian War; Italian Eritrea)
- 11 September 1952: **UN federation with Ethiopia** — Under UN Resolution 390 A (V) of 1950, Eritrea becomes an autonomous unit federated with Ethiopia, keeping its own parliament, flag and control of internal affairs. (from the Eritrea chronicle; sources: Federation of Ethiopia and Eritrea; A Guide to the United States' History of Recognition: Eritrea)
- 24 May 1991: **EPLF captures Asmara** — As the Derg regime collapses in Ethiopia, EPLF forces enter Asmara, winning de facto independence and forming a provisional government. (from the Eritrea chronicle; sources: Eritrean People's Liberation Front; Eritrean War of Independence)
- May 1991: **Fall of the Derg and a new federal order** — The EPRDF captures Addis Ababa and Mengistu flees into exile, ending the civil war and the Derg; a new ethnic-federal constitution follows, and Eritrea votes for independence in 1993, leaving Ethiopia landlocked. (from the Ethiopia chronicle; sources: Fall of the Derg regime; 1993 Eritrean independence referendum)
- May 1998 - June 2000: **Border war with Ethiopia** — A dispute over Badme escalates into a full-scale war that kills tens of thousands and displaces more than a million before a ceasefire. (from the Eritrea chronicle; sources: Eritrean-Ethiopian War (1998 invasion of Ethiopia); Algiers Agreement (2000))
- 12 December 2000: **Algiers Agreement** — Eritrea and Ethiopia sign the Algiers peace agreement; a boundary commission later awards Badme to Eritrea, but Ethiopia refuses to withdraw, leaving a frozen stand-off. (from the Eritrea chronicle; sources: Algiers Agreement (2000))
- 9 July 2018: **Peace with Ethiopia** — Isaias Afwerki and Abiy Ahmed sign a declaration formally ending the border war, though internal repression in Eritrea continues largely unchanged. (from the Eritrea chronicle; sources: Eritrea-Ethiopia Peace Deal Offers Hope for Reform)
