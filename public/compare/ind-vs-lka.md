## Entangled histories (5 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- from c. 2nd century BCE: **Hydraulic civilisation and South Indian invasions** — Anuradhapura's kings build vast reservoir-and-channel systems to irrigate the dry plain, while recurrent invasions from South India culminate in the Chola conquest and the abandonment of the capital around 1017. (from the Sri Lanka chronicle; sources: Anuradhapura, Sri Lanka; Sacred City of Anuradhapura)
- 1830s onward: **Plantation economy and Tamil labour migration** — British coffee and later tea estates transform the highlands; indentured South Indian Tamil labourers are brought in to work them, forming the Indian Tamil community of the plantation districts. (from the Sri Lanka chronicle; sources: Sri Lanka: History)
- 1949: **Indian Tamil plantation workers disenfranchised** — The estate workforce brought from South India is stripped of the vote a year after independence — the opening move of a nationalism that alienated the Tamil minority. (from the Sri Lanka chronicle; sources: Sri Lanka country profile)
- 1987: **The Indo-Lanka accords and the Indian peacekeepers** — Accords create new councils for the Tamil north and east and bring in an Indian peacekeeping force, which spends three years bogged down in the fighting it came to end. (from the Sri Lanka chronicle; sources: Sri Lanka country profile)
- 1990: **Indian withdrawal and the expulsion of northern Muslims** — Indian troops leave and the war escalates; the LTTE expels thousands of Muslims from the northern districts. (from the Sri Lanka chronicle; sources: Sri Lanka country profile)
