## Entangled histories (2 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 1921: **Liberal constitution of 1921** — Following the First World War, Liechtenstein adopted a new constitution that combined the prince with an elected Landtag and instruments of direct democracy, modernising the state as it reoriented toward Switzerland. (from the Liechtenstein chronicle; sources: The History of the Princely House of Liechtenstein; Liechtenstein: History (Alpine, Principality, Sovereignty))
- 1923–1924: **Customs union with Switzerland and the Swiss franc** — A customs treaty signed in 1923 and in force from 1 January 1924 joined Liechtenstein and Switzerland into a single customs and economic area; Liechtenstein adopted the Swiss franc, binding its economy to its western neighbour. (from the Liechtenstein chronicle; sources: Advantages of the customs and monetary union with Switzerland; History of Liechtenstein; Liechtenstein: History (Alpine, Principality, Sovereignty))
