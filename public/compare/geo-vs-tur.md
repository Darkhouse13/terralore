## Entangled histories (2 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 24 July 1783: **Treaty of Georgievsk** — King Erekle II of Kartli-Kakheti placed his kingdom under Russian protection, ceding control of foreign affairs in exchange for guarantees of its integrity and dynasty against Persia and the Ottomans. (from the Georgia chronicle; sources: Treaty of Georgievsk (1783); Georgia: Turkish and Persian domination)
