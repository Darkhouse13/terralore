## Entangled histories (3 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- c. 1332: **Ifat broken by Ethiopia; rise of Adal** — The Ethiopian emperor Amda Seyon defeats Ifat; the Sultanate of Adal succeeds it as the leading Muslim power contesting the Christian highlands. (from the Djibouti chronicle; sources: History of Djibouti; Djibouti (country and history))
- 1529: **Adal's invasion of the highlands** — Adal forces under Ahmad ibn Ibrahim al-Ghazi ('Gran') invade Christian Ethiopia, the high point of the coastal Muslim states' power before Adal's later decline. (from the Djibouti chronicle; sources: Djibouti (country and history); Djibouti)
- 1917: **Railway reaches Addis Ababa** — The line is completed to Addis Ababa, giving landlocked Ethiopia its main outlet to the sea and confirming Djibouti as the entrepôt for Ethiopian trade. (from the Djibouti chronicle; sources: Ethio-Djibouti Railways; 100-year-old Ethiopia's French-built railway remains vital for residents; Djibouti (country and history))
