- 1988: **Nagorno-Karabakh dispute erupts** — The regional legislature of Nagorno-Karabakh, an Armenian-majority enclave within Soviet Azerbaijan, voted to join Armenia, triggering a conflict that escalated into open war. (from the Armenia chronicle; sources: Nagorno-Karabakh)
