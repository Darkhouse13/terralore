## Entangled histories (4 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 1916: **Belgium occupies Rwanda** — Belgian forces take Rwanda from Germany during the First World War; from 1922 it is governed with Burundi as Ruanda-Urundi under a League of Nations mandate. (from the Rwanda chronicle; sources: History of Rwanda; Divided by Ethnicity)
- 1 July 1962: **Independence from Belgium** — Rwanda becomes a sovereign republic with Grégoire Kayibanda as first president, ending Belgian rule of Ruanda-Urundi; Burundi gains independence the same day. (from the Rwanda chronicle; sources: History of Rwanda; Rise of Rwanda)
- 1 July 1962: **Independence under the monarchy** — Burundi becomes independent from Belgium as a constitutional monarchy under Mwami Mwambutsa IV, separating from Rwanda and retaining its throne. (from the Burundi chronicle; sources: History of Burundi; Burundi: History; Mwambutsa IV of Burundi)
- 6 April 1994: **Habyarimana's plane shot down** — A surface-to-air missile downs the plane carrying President Habyarimana and Burundi's president over Kigali, killing all aboard; extremists seize on it to unleash the genocide. (from the Rwanda chronicle; sources: Massacre of the Tutsi Minority; Outreach Programme on the 1994 Genocide against the Tutsi in Rwanda and the United Nations; Juvénal Habyarimana)
