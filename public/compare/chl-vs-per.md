## Entangled histories (5 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- c. 1471-1493: **Empire at its height** — Under Pachacuti's successors the Inca extend Tawantinsuyu from present-day Colombia to Chile and Argentina, bound by roads, storehouses and the mita labour system. (from the Peru chronicle; sources: Pachacuti Inca Yupanqui; City of Cuzco)
- 1536-1537: **Almagro's expedition turns back** — Diego de Almagro leads the first Spanish entry into Chile as far as the Maule River; finding no gold or empire, the expedition returns to Peru, but the Spanish first clash with the Araucanians. (from the Chile chronicle; sources: Diego de Almagro; History of Chile)
- 1879-1883: **The War of the Pacific** — A dispute over nitrate taxes leads Chile to war with Peru and Bolivia; Chilean victories win the nitrate-rich Atacama and leave Bolivia landlocked. (from the Chile chronicle; sources: War of the Pacific; History of Chile)
- 1879-1883: **War of the Pacific** — Allied with Bolivia, Peru fights Chile over the nitrate-rich Atacama; Chilean forces occupy Lima in 1881 and Peru is defeated. (from the Peru chronicle; sources: War of the Pacific; Peru: History)
- 20 Oct 1883: **Treaty of Ancon** — Peru cedes Tarapaca to Chile and loses control of Tacna and Arica, a humiliating settlement whose final terms were not fixed until 1929. (from the Peru chronicle; sources: War of the Pacific)
