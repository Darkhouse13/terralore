## Entangled histories (4 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 20th century: **Migrant labour to South Africa** — With little arable land, large numbers of Basotho men become migrant workers on South African farms and gold mines, and remittances become central to the economy. (from the Lesotho chronicle; sources: Migration, Remittances and 'Development' in Lesotho; History of Lesotho)
- 1910: **Kept out of the Union of South Africa** — On the formation of the Union, Basutoland is excluded as a British High Commission Territory rather than incorporated into South Africa. (from the Lesotho chronicle; sources: Southern Africa, 1899–1945; History of Lesotho)
- January 1986: **Military coup and the water treaty** — A military coup overthrows Jonathan; in the same year the new government signs the treaty launching the Lesotho Highlands Water Project with South Africa. (from the Lesotho chronicle; sources: History of Lesotho; The Lesotho Highlands Water Project is 40 years old and going strong)
- 1998–2002: **Katse and Mohale dams completed** — The Katse Dam (Phase 1A) and then the Mohale Dam are completed, sending Lesotho's mountain water to South Africa's Vaal system in exchange for royalties. (from the Lesotho chronicle; sources: The Lesotho Highlands Water Project is 40 years old and going strong; Lesotho; Lesotho Highlands Water Project II — environmental and social assessment)
