- 1916: **The 1916 revolt (Urkun)** — A decree conscripting Central Asians for wartime labour triggered a revolt across Russian Turkestan; in the Kyrgyz lands it was suppressed with great violence, and tens of thousands fled toward China, with heavy loss of life. Casualty figures remain estimates. (from the Kyrgyzstan chronicle; sources: Kyrgyzstan: Victims Of 1916 'Urkun' Tragedy Commemorated; Kyrgyzstan: A Country Study (in Kazakstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, and Uzbekistan: Country Studies))
