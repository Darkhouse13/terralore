- 10th–12th centuries: **Silk Road towns of the south flourish** — Caravan cities such as Otrar, Taraz, Sauran, and Yasi prosper on the trade between China and the Mediterranean, becoming centres of commerce, craft, and Islamic learning along the southern Kazakh steppe. (from the Kazakhstan chronicle; sources: History of Kazakhstan; Kazakhstan)
- 1771: **Ablai Khan elected ruler of the Kazakhs** — Ablai (Abylai) is elected khan of the Kazakhs and balances between the Russian and Qing empires to preserve a degree of independence; he endures as a national symbol of Kazakh sovereignty. (from the Kazakhstan chronicle; sources: Abylai the King: A Warrior, Diplomat and Patriot; History of Kazakhstan)
