## Entangled histories (3 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- c. 17th century: **The Maravi Empire at its peak** — In the seventeenth century the confederacy administered a broad domain reaching across parts of modern Malawi, Mozambique and Zambia, trading ivory and iron with Portuguese and Swahili merchants on the coast. (from the Malawi chronicle; sources: Maravi Confederacy; Maravi; The Kingdom of Maravi)
- c. 1820s–1850s: **Ngoni and Yao migrations reshape the region** — Ngoni warrior bands fleeing the Mfecane settled around Lake Nyasa, while the Yao migrated from northern Mozambique and the Tumbuka occupied the northern highlands, overlaying new peoples on the older Chewa chiefdoms. (from the Malawi chronicle; sources: Malawi (country overview and history); History of Malawi)
- February 1915: **Chilembwe killed** — John Chilembwe was shot dead as he fled toward Mozambique; though the rising failed, he became an enduring symbol of Malawian nationalism and is honoured as a national martyr. (from the Malawi chronicle; sources: John Chilembwe; John Chilembwe (ca. 1871–1915))
