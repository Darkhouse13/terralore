## Entangled histories (3 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 1608: **The House of Liechtenstein raised to princely rank** — Karl von Liechtenstein received the hereditary dignity of an imperial prince in 1608, confirmed for his brothers soon after, raising the wealthy Austrian dynasty to the highest rank of the nobility. (from the Liechtenstein chronicle; sources: The History of the Princely House of Liechtenstein; The History of the Family and the Collections)
- 1815: **Membership of the German Confederation** — After Napoleon's defeat, Liechtenstein joined the German Confederation created at the Congress of Vienna, remaining within it until 1866 while keeping close dynastic ties to Austria. (from the Liechtenstein chronicle; sources: Liechtenstein: History (Alpine, Principality, Sovereignty))
- 1842: **A prince visits at last** — Prince Alois II became the first reigning prince to set foot in Liechtenstein, more than a century after its foundation, the dynasty having until then governed entirely from its Austrian and Bohemian seats. (from the Liechtenstein chronicle; sources: How the Principality of Liechtenstein came into being; Liechtenstein: History (Alpine, Principality, Sovereignty))
