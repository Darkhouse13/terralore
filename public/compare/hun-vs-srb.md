## Entangled histories (3 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 1402–1459: **The Serbian Despotate** — A reduced Serbian state survived as the Despotate, ruled latterly from Smederevo and balancing between the Ottomans and Hungary, prolonging Serb statehood for another half-century. (from the Serbia chronicle; sources: Serbia: History)
- 1912–1913: **The Balkan Wars** — Serbia drove the Ottomans from most of their European lands and, after the Second Balkan War, gained Kosovo, Metohija and much of Macedonia, nearly doubling its territory and alarming Austria-Hungary. (from the Serbia chronicle; sources: Serbia: History)
- 28 Jun 1914: **Assassination at Sarajevo** — The Bosnian Serb Gavrilo Princip assassinated Archduke Franz Ferdinand in Sarajevo; Austria-Hungary blamed Serbia, and its declaration of war triggered the First World War. (from the Serbia chronicle; sources: Serbia: History)
