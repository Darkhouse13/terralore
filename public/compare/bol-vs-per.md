## Entangled histories (5 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 1538-1559: **Spanish conquest of Charcas** — Spanish forces subdue the highlands of Upper Peru, organising them as the Audiencia of Charcas within the Viceroyalty of Peru. (from the Bolivia chronicle; sources: Bolivia: Early period; Bolivia: A Country Study)
- 1809 CE: **Uprisings at Chuquisaca and La Paz** — Revolts in Upper Peru in 1809, sparked by the Spanish crisis under Napoleon, are remembered among the first independence movements in the Americas. (from the Bolivia chronicle; sources: Bolivia: Early period; Bolivia: A Country Study)
- 6 Aug 1825: **Republic of Bolivia proclaimed** — A congress at Chuquisaca declares the independence of Upper Peru as a republic named Bolivia in honour of Simon Bolivar, with Sucre soon its first president. (from the Bolivia chronicle; sources: Simon Bolivar: Bolivia; Antonio Jose de Sucre)
- 1879-1883: **War of the Pacific** — Chile defeats Bolivia and its ally Peru and annexes Bolivia's coast; Bolivia loses its Pacific province of Antofagasta and becomes landlocked. (from the Bolivia chronicle; sources: War of the Pacific; Bolivia: A Country Study)
- 1879-1883: **War of the Pacific** — Allied with Bolivia, Peru fights Chile over the nitrate-rich Atacama; Chilean forces occupy Lima in 1881 and Peru is defeated. (from the Peru chronicle; sources: War of the Pacific; Peru: History)
