## Entangled histories (3 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 1607: **The French crown becomes co-prince** — After the rights of Foix passed through Navarre to Henry IV, an edict confirmed the King of France and the Bishop of Urgell as Andorra's co-princes; the French role later passed to the head of state. (from the Andorra chronicle; sources: Andorra: Countries; Andorra: History, Facts & Points of Interest)
- 1789–1815: **Neutrality through revolution and war** — Through the French Revolution and Napoleonic Wars the co-principality survived; the French co-princely role transferred to each new French regime while Andorra guarded its neutrality. (from the Andorra chronicle; sources: Andorra: History, Facts & Points of Interest; Andorra: Member state)
- 1939–1945: **Neutral in the Second World War** — Andorra preserved its neutrality through the Second World War, its mountain passes serving as a quiet crossing-point between Vichy France and Spain. (from the Andorra chronicle; sources: Andorra: History, Facts & Points of Interest; Andorra: Member state)
