## Entangled histories (4 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 1913: **Kuwait's separate status confirmed** — Anglo-Ottoman negotiations recognized Kuwait as an autonomous district under British protection, distinguishing it from the Ottoman territories that would later form Iraq. (from the Kuwait chronicle; sources: Kuwait: History; Persian Gulf States: Country Studies (Kuwait))
- 2 August 1990: **Iraq invades and annexes Kuwait** — About one hundred thousand Iraqi troops overran Kuwait in hours; Saddam Hussein declared the country annexed, and the ruling family fled to Saudi Arabia. (from the Kuwait chronicle; sources: The Gulf War, 1991 (Milestones in the History of U.S. Foreign Relations); The First Gulf War (Short History))
- August-November 1990: **The UN condemns and authorizes force** — The Security Council passed Resolution 660 demanding withdrawal and Resolution 678 authorizing all necessary means to expel Iraq if it did not leave by 15 January 1991. (from the Kuwait chronicle; sources: The Gulf War, 1991 (Milestones in the History of U.S. Foreign Relations))
- 1990–1991: **Invasion of Kuwait and the Persian Gulf War** — Iraq invades and annexes Kuwait in August 1990; a United States-led coalition of many nations expels Iraqi forces in early 1991, after which Iraq is placed under severe international sanctions. (from the Iraq chronicle; sources: Persian Gulf War)
