## Entangled histories (3 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 13–14 Sep 1515: **Battle of Marignano** — A French army under Francis I decisively defeated the Swiss near Milan, the first great Swiss defeat in two hundred years; the Confederates withdrew from Italy, a retreat traditionally seen as the dawn of Swiss neutrality. (from the Switzerland chronicle; sources: Neutrality; Switzerland: History)
- 1798: **French invasion and the Helvetic Republic** — Revolutionary France conquered Switzerland and replaced the old Confederation with the Helvetic Republic, a centralised unitary state that abolished cantonal sovereignty and the privileges of the patrician towns. (from the Switzerland chronicle; sources: Helvetic Republic; The History of Switzerland)
- 19 Feb 1803: **Napoleon's Act of Mediation** — After years of factional strife Napoleon imposed the Act of Mediation, restoring a confederal system and creating six new full cantons, including Vaud and Ticino, while keeping Switzerland tied to France. (from the Switzerland chronicle; sources: Helvetic Republic)
