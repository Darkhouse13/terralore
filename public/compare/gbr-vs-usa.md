## Entangled histories (6 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 1607: **Founding of Jamestown** — English settlers established Jamestown in Virginia, the first permanent English settlement in North America, financed by the Virginia Company of London. (from the United States chronicle; sources: Jamestown Colony)
- August 1619: **First Africans landed in Virginia** — An English ship brought roughly twenty enslaved Africans to Point Comfort, Virginia, marking the beginning of African slavery in the English mainland colonies. (from the United States chronicle; sources: Arrival of the First Africans in 1619)
- 1754–1763: **French and Indian War** — Britain's victory in the North American theatre of the Seven Years' War expelled France from the continent but left Britain heavily indebted, prompting new colonial taxes. (from the United States chronicle; sources: Abraham Lincoln and Emancipation; U.S. History Collections)
- 4 July 1776: **Declaration of Independence** — The Second Continental Congress adopted the Declaration of Independence, formally announcing the separation of the thirteen colonies from Great Britain. (from the United States chronicle; sources: The Declaration of Independence)
- 1783: **Loss of the American colonies** — The Treaty of Paris recognised the independence of the United States, ending the American Revolutionary War and stripping Britain of its thirteen mainland colonies. (from the United Kingdom chronicle; sources: The British Library: history and collections)
- 1783: **Treaty of Paris** — Great Britain recognised the independence of the United States, ending the Revolutionary War and ceding territory east of the Mississippi. (from the United States chronicle; sources: Abraham Lincoln and Emancipation; U.S. History Collections)
