## Entangled histories (2 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 1888–1890: **Rudd Concession and the Pioneer Column** — Lobengula signed the 1888 Rudd Concession granting mining rights to Rhodes's agents; Rhodes won a royal charter in 1889 and in 1890 the British South Africa Company's Pioneer Column occupied Mashonaland, raising the flag at Fort Salisbury on 13 September. (from the Zimbabwe chronicle; sources: Zimbabwe: History; Lobengula; Pioneer Column)
- 1893: **First Matabele War** — Company forces invaded and destroyed the Ndebele kingdom; Lobengula fled and soon died, and the British South Africa Company annexed Matabeleland. (from the Zimbabwe chronicle; sources: Lobengula; Zimbabwe: History)
