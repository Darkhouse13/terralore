## Entangled histories (5 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 1772–1795: **The Partitions of Poland** — In three stages (1772, 1793, 1795) Russia, Prussia and Austria divided the Commonwealth among themselves, despite the reforming Constitution of 3 May 1791, ending Polish statehood for 123 years. (from the Poland chronicle; sources: Partitions of Poland; Constitution of 3 May 1791)
- 1 Sep 1939: **German and Soviet invasion of Poland** — Nazi Germany invaded on 1 September, starting the Second World War in Europe; the Soviet Union invaded from the east on 17 September, and the two powers again partitioned Poland. (from the Poland chronicle; sources: Invasion of Poland, Fall 1939; Invasion of Poland (1939))
- 1 Sep 1939: **Invasion of Poland** — Germany invaded Poland, igniting the Second World War in Europe and beginning six years of conquest, occupation and total war. (from the Germany chronicle; sources: The Holocaust and World War II: Key Dates)
- 1940–1945: **Auschwitz-Birkenau and the Holocaust on Polish soil** — Germany operated its largest concentration and extermination camp at Auschwitz-Birkenau, where some 1.1 million people, overwhelmingly Jews, were murdered as part of the genocide carried out across occupied Poland. (from the Poland chronicle; sources: Auschwitz Birkenau German Nazi Concentration and Extermination Camp (1940-1945); Polish Victims)
- 1 Aug 1944: **The Warsaw Uprising** — The Polish Home Army rose against the German occupiers in Warsaw; after 63 days the rising was crushed, much of the city was destroyed and tens of thousands of civilians were killed. (from the Poland chronicle; sources: Polish Victims)
