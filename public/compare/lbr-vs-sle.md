## Entangled histories (3 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 23 March 1991: **Civil war begins** — The Revolutionary United Front under Foday Sankoh, backed by Liberia's Charles Taylor, invaded from Liberia, beginning an eleven-year civil war marked by atrocities and funded by 'blood diamonds'. (from the Sierra Leone chronicle; sources: The Sierra Leone Civil War (1991–2002); Foday Sankoh)
- August 2003: **Taylor resigns; Accra peace agreement** — With rebels besieging Monrovia and an international indictment over Sierra Leone hanging over him, Charles Taylor resigned and went into exile; a comprehensive peace agreement in Accra ended the war and a UN mission, UNMIL, deployed. (from the Liberia chronicle; sources: Second Liberian Civil War (1999–2003); Charles Taylor; Second Liberian Civil War)
- 26 April 2012: **Taylor convicted of war crimes** — The Special Court for Sierra Leone convicted Charles Taylor of aiding and abetting war crimes and crimes against humanity in Sierra Leone and sentenced him to 50 years — the first former head of state convicted by an international tribunal since Nuremberg. (from the Liberia chronicle; sources: Sierra Leone: Landmark Conviction of Liberian Ex-President; Special Court sentences former Liberian leader Charles Taylor to 50 years in prison)
