## Entangled histories (3 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- c. 13th–17th centuries: **Flourishing of Benin and Oyo** — The Edo kingdom of Benin and the Yoruba empire of Oyo rose as major forest powers, Benin renowned for its court art and Oyo for the cavalry empire of its Alaafin. (from the Nigeria chronicle; sources: Oyo empire; Benin Bronzes; Nigeria (country overview and history))
- 16th century onward: **The Atlantic slave trade** — European demand drew the Bights of Benin and Biafra into the transatlantic slave trade; ports such as Bonny and Old Calabar became major export points, and vast numbers of people were deported in conditions of extreme violence. (from the Nigeria chronicle; sources: Trans-Atlantic Slave Trade Database (Estimates); Bight of Biafra; Nigeria (country overview and history))
- February 1897: **British sack of Benin City** — A British punitive expedition destroyed Benin City, exiled the Oba, and looted thousands of the Benin Bronzes, dispersing centuries of court art to foreign museums and collectors. (from the Nigeria chronicle; sources: Benin Bronzes; The Benin Bronzes; The British raid on Benin 1897)
