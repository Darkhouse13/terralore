## Entangled histories (4 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 1864–1865: **The Duar War with British India** — After a failed British mission and disputes over the southern duars, Britain and Bhutan fight a five-month war that ends in Bhutanese defeat. (from the Bhutan chronicle; sources: Bhutan — History; Nepal and Bhutan: Country Studies)
- 11 Nov 1865: **Treaty of Sinchula** — Bhutan cedes the Bengal and Assam duars to British India in exchange for an annual subsidy, fixing its modern southern border and binding it to the Raj. (from the Bhutan chronicle; sources: Bhutan — History; Nepal and Bhutan: Country Studies)
- 1949: **The treaty with independent India** — Delhi guarantees non-interference in Bhutan's internal affairs while retaining influence over its foreign relations — the terms of British Punakha, inherited by a new republic. (from the Bhutan chronicle; sources: Bhutan country profile)
- 2007: **The treaty with India revised** — A new agreement gives Bhutan more say over its own foreign and defence policy, loosening a constraint carried from 1910 through 1949. (from the Bhutan chronicle; sources: Bhutan country profile)
