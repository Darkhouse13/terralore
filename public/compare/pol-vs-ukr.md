## Entangled histories (7 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 14th century: **Rus' lands pass to Lithuania and Poland** — Following the collapse of Galicia-Volhynia, most of the former Rus' lands were absorbed by the Grand Duchy of Lithuania, while Galicia in the west passed to the Kingdom of Poland. (from the Ukraine chronicle; sources: History of Ukraine)
- 1 Jul 1569: **Union of Lublin brings Ukraine into the Commonwealth** — The union of Poland and Lithuania placed the bulk of the Ukrainian lands within the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth, intensifying pressures of serfdom and Catholicism on the Orthodox population. (from the Ukraine chronicle; sources: History of Ukraine)
- 1648: **Khmelnytsky's uprising** — Bohdan Khmelnytsky led the Zaporozhian Cossacks, allied with the Crimean Tatars, in a great rebellion against Polish rule, founding a self-governing Cossack Hetmanate in the Ukrainian lands. (from the Ukraine chronicle; sources: Bohdan Khmelnytsky; Ukraine: The Cossacks)
- 1654: **Council of Pereiaslav** — Khmelnytsky and the Cossack assembly swore allegiance to the tsar, placing the Hetmanate under Muscovite protection while seeking to retain its autonomy, and drawing Muscovy into war with Poland. (from the Ukraine chronicle; sources: Bohdan Khmelnytsky; History of Ukraine)
- 1667: **Partition along the Dnieper** — The Truce of Andrusovo and later settlements divided the Ukrainian lands between Russia and Poland, with the left bank and Kyiv under Russian control and the right bank remaining Polish. (from the Ukraine chronicle; sources: History of Ukraine; Bohdan Khmelnytsky)
- 1772–1795: **Galicia to Austria, right bank to Russia** — The partitions of Poland transferred Galicia to the Habsburg Empire in 1772 and most of the right-bank Ukrainian lands to the Russian Empire by 1795, dividing the Ukrainian people between two empires. (from the Ukraine chronicle; sources: History of Ukraine)
- 1921: **Defeat and Soviet incorporation** — After years of war between Bolshevik, Polish, White and Ukrainian forces, the People's Republic was defeated; most Ukrainian lands were incorporated into the Soviet state, while Galicia passed to Poland. (from the Ukraine chronicle; sources: Ukraine: World War I and the struggle for independence; History of Ukraine)
