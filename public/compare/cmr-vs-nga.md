## Entangled histories (3 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- c. 1000 BCE: **The Bantu expansion begins** — The Grassfields of the Cameroon-Nigeria borderlands are widely held to lie near the homeland of the Bantu languages, whose speakers spread from this region across central and southern Africa. (from the Cameroon chronicle; sources: History of Cameroon; Cameroon (country overview and history))
- 1919–1922: **Partition into French and British mandates** — The League of Nations divided the former German colony between France, which took the larger French Cameroun, and Britain, which administered the Northern and Southern Cameroons alongside Nigeria. (from the Cameroon chronicle; sources: Cameroon (country overview and history); A Guide to the History of Recognition: Cameroon; History of Cameroon)
- 11 February 1961: **The British Cameroons plebiscite** — In a UN-supervised vote the Northern Cameroons chose to join Nigeria while the Southern Cameroons chose to reunite with the Republic of Cameroon. (from the Cameroon chronicle; sources: 1961 British Cameroons referendum; Cameroon (country overview and history))
