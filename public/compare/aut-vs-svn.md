## Entangled histories (3 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 1335: **Habsburgs acquire Carniola and Carinthia** — Carinthia and Carniola passed to the Habsburg dynasty, which already held Styria, bringing nearly all the Slovene lands under the rule that would last about six centuries. (from the Slovenia chronicle; sources: Slovenia: The Middle Ages; History of Slovenia)
- 1815: **Austria regains the Slovene lands** — After Napoleon's defeat the Congress of Vienna restored Habsburg rule over the Illyrian territories, returning the Slovene lands to the Austrian Empire. (from the Slovenia chronicle; sources: History of Slovenia; Ljubljana)
- 10 Oct 1920: **The Carinthian Plebiscite** — In the disputed southern Carinthian basin, a majority of about 59 per cent voted to remain with Austria rather than join Yugoslavia, leaving a Slovene minority on the Austrian side of the border. (from the Slovenia chronicle; sources: Carinthian Plebiscite, 1920; Slovenia since 1918)
