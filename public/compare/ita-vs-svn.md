## Entangled histories (4 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 1920: **Treaty of Rapallo and the Italian border** — Under the Treaty of Rapallo, Italy gained the Slovene Littoral, the Karst and parts of Inner Carniola, subjecting a large Slovene population to Italian and later Fascist rule. (from the Slovenia chronicle; sources: Slovenia since 1918)
- Apr 1941: **Axis partition of Slovenia** — Following the invasion of Yugoslavia, the Axis powers carved up the Slovene lands among Germany, Italy and Hungary, while a Liberation Front founded in Ljubljana launched armed resistance. (from the Slovenia chronicle; sources: Slovenia since 1918; History of Slovenia)
- 5 Oct 1954: **London Memorandum on Trieste** — The United States, United Kingdom, Italy and Yugoslavia agreed to divide the Free Territory of Trieste, placing Zone A under Italian and Zone B under Yugoslav administration. (from the Slovenia chronicle; sources: The Trieste Settlement, October 1954 (London Memorandum); Treaty of Osimo (1975) and the Trieste settlement)
- 10 Nov 1975: **Treaty of Osimo** — Italy and Yugoslavia signed the Treaty of Osimo, making the partition of the former Free Territory of Trieste definitive and settling the long-disputed western border. (from the Slovenia chronicle; sources: Treaty of Osimo (1975) and the Trieste settlement)
