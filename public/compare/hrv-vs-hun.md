## Entangled histories (4 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 1102: **Personal union with Hungary** — King Coloman of Hungary was crowned king of Croatia and Dalmatia, joining the crowns in a personal union; Croatian tradition records the terms in the Pacta conventa, under which Croatia kept its Sabor and its ban. (from the Croatia chronicle; sources: Croatia in Europe through the Ages: Development of the State; Croatia: History)
- 1847: **Croatian made the official language** — The Croatian Sabor adopted Croatian in place of Latin as the official language, a milestone of the national revival and a response to the imposition of Hungarian. (from the Croatia chronicle; sources: Croatia: The Croatian national revival)
- 1868: **The Croatian-Hungarian Settlement (Nagodba)** — Within the Dual Monarchy created in 1867, the Nagodba granted Croatia-Slavonia limited autonomy under its own ban and Sabor, though real authority remained with Budapest. (from the Croatia chronicle; sources: Croatia: History)
- 1 Dec 1918: **Entry into the Kingdom of Serbs, Croats and Slovenes** — After the collapse of Austria-Hungary, the Croatian lands joined Serbia and Montenegro in the new South Slav kingdom proclaimed on 1 December 1918, later renamed Yugoslavia. (from the Croatia chronicle; sources: Kingdom of Serbs, Croats, and Slovenes; Croatia: History)
