## Entangled histories (8 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 1330: **Basarab I founds Wallachia** — The voivode Basarab I defeated King Charles I of Hungary at Posada, winning recognition of Wallachia's autonomy and establishing the first Romanian principality. (from the Romania chronicle; sources: Walachia)
- c. 1359: **Bogdan I founds Moldavia** — The Vlach voivode Bogdan I crossed the Carpathians from Maramures and wrenched Moldavia from Hungarian vassalage, founding the second Romanian principality. (from the Romania chronicle; sources: Walachia)
- 1916: **Romania enters the First World War** — Romania joined the Allies in 1916 seeking the Romanian lands of Austria-Hungary, especially Transylvania; despite early defeats, the collapse of the empires opened the way to union. (from the Romania chronicle; sources: Treaty of Trianon)
- 1918: **Greater Romania** — With Bessarabia, Bukovina and Transylvania joined to the kingdom, Romania nearly doubled in size, gaining large Hungarian and German minorities within its enlarged borders. (from the Romania chronicle; sources: Treaty of Trianon; Romania)
- 4 Jun 1920: **The Treaty of Trianon** — Hungary lost about two-thirds of its territory and population, leaving roughly three million ethnic Hungarians outside the new borders in Romania, Czechoslovakia, Yugoslavia and elsewhere; the trauma shaped Hungarian politics for a century. (from the Hungary chronicle; sources: Treaty of Trianon)
- 4 Jun 1920: **Treaty of Trianon** — The Treaty of Trianon formally transferred Transylvania and part of the Banat from Hungary to Romania, stripping Hungary of about two-thirds of its territory and leaving a lasting dispute. (from the Romania chronicle; sources: Treaty of Trianon)
- 30 Aug 1940: **Second Vienna Award** — Germany and Italy awarded Hungary northern Transylvania from Romania, deepening Hungary's dependence on the Axis powers in its quest to reverse Trianon. (from the Hungary chronicle; sources: Treaty of Trianon)
- 30 Aug 1940: **Second Vienna Award and Southern Dobruja** — Under German and Italian arbitration Romania ceded northern Transylvania to Hungary; in September southern Dobruja was ceded to Bulgaria, costing Romania about 30% of its territory and people. (from the Romania chronicle; sources: Romania)
