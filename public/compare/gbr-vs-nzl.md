## Entangled histories (4 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 6 February 1840: **Treaty of Waitangi signed** — The Crown and Māori rangatira signed the Treaty of Waitangi, founding British New Zealand; its differing English and Māori texts — over sovereignty versus governorship — left a meaning contested to this day. (from the New Zealand chronicle; sources: Differences between the texts of the Treaty of Waitangi; Te Tiriti o Waitangi — the Treaty of Waitangi; New Zealand (country))
- 1882: **First refrigerated meat shipment** — The first cargo of frozen meat sailed from New Zealand to Britain, launching the refrigerated export trade in meat and dairy that would underpin the country's economy for a century. (from the New Zealand chronicle; sources: New Zealand (country))
- 26 September 1907: **New Zealand becomes a Dominion** — New Zealand's status was raised from colony to Dominion within the British Empire, a symbolic advance toward nationhood that left its close ties to Britain largely intact. (from the New Zealand chronicle; sources: Statute of Westminster enacted; New Zealand (country))
- 25 November 1947: **Statute of Westminster adopted** — New Zealand adopted the Statute of Westminster, ending the British Parliament's power to legislate for it and confirming the country's full legal independence — the last Dominion to do so. (from the New Zealand chronicle; sources: Statute of Westminster enacted; New Zealand (country))
