## Entangled histories (8 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 14 Aug 1947: **Independence of Pakistan** — Pakistan becomes independent under the Indian Independence Act, with Jinnah as Governor-General and Liaquat Ali Khan as prime minister, comprising West and East Pakistan. (from the Pakistan chronicle; sources: Mohammed Ali Jinnah; Indian Independence Act)
- 1947: **Partition creates East Pakistan** — The partition of British India makes Muslim-majority eastern Bengal into East Pakistan, a wing of the new state separated from its western half by a thousand miles of India. (from the Bangladesh chronicle; sources: History of Bangladesh)
- 21 Feb 1952: **The Bengali Language Movement** — Students in Dhaka demanding that Bengali be a state language of Pakistan are shot by police; the martyrs become foundational to Bengali nationalism and, later, to UNESCO's International Mother Language Day. (from the Bangladesh chronicle; sources: Language Movement; International Mother Language Day)
- 1966: **The Awami League's Six-Point demand** — Sheikh Mujibur Rahman's Awami League issues a six-point programme for the political and economic autonomy of East Pakistan, the charter of Bengali self-determination. (from the Bangladesh chronicle; sources: Mujibur Rahman; Awami League)
- Dec 1970: **Awami League wins the election** — The Awami League secures 167 of the 169 East Pakistani seats and a National Assembly majority; West Pakistan's refusal to hand over power ignites the crisis that leads to war. (from the Bangladesh chronicle; sources: Mujibur Rahman; Awami League)
- 26 Mar 1971: **Declaration of independence** — After the Pakistani army launches Operation Searchlight on 25 March, Sheikh Mujibur Rahman proclaims Bangladesh's independence on 26 March, beginning the Liberation War. (from the Bangladesh chronicle; sources: Bangladesh Liberation War; Mujibur Rahman)
- 16 Dec 1971: **Loss of East Pakistan and birth of Bangladesh** — After a brutal war and Indian intervention, Pakistani forces surrender at Dhaka and East Pakistan becomes the independent state of Bangladesh, dismembering the country. (from the Pakistan chronicle; sources: Bangladesh Liberation War; 1971 India-Pakistan War)
- 16 Dec 1971: **Victory and the birth of Bangladesh** — After a nine-month war in which possibly a million Bengalis were killed and ten million fled to India, Indian forces intervene and the Pakistani army surrenders at Dhaka, founding the People's Republic of Bangladesh. (from the Bangladesh chronicle; sources: Bangladesh Liberation War)
