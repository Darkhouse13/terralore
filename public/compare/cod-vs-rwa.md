## Entangled histories (3 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 1994 onward: **Refugee crisis and the Congo wars** — Over a million Hutu flee to camps in Zaire and beyond; the presence of armed génocidaires there draws Rwanda into the Congo wars from 1996, which kill millions across the region. (from the Rwanda chronicle; sources: Outreach Programme on the 1994 Genocide against the Tutsi in Rwanda — Historical Background; History of Rwanda)
- 1996–1997: **First Congo War** — A Rwanda- and Uganda-backed rebellion under Laurent-Désiré Kabila advances across the country against the collapsing Mobutu regime. (from the DR Congo chronicle; sources: Kabila, Laurent-Désiré (1939–2001); Mobutu Sese Seko)
- Aug 1998: **Second Congo War begins** — Kabila breaks with Rwanda and Uganda; nine nations and many militias are drawn into 'Africa's World War,' the deadliest conflict since 1945. (from the DR Congo chronicle; sources: Second Congo War)
