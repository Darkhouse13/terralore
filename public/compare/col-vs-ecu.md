## Entangled histories (5 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 1717, restored 1739: **Viceroyalty of New Granada** — Spain governs the territory through the Viceroyalty of New Granada, capital Bogota, encompassing modern Colombia, Panama, Ecuador, and Venezuela. (from the Colombia chronicle; sources: Viceroyalty of New Granada)
- 1821 CE: **Congress of Cucuta** — The republic of Gran Colombia is formally organised, uniting New Granada, Venezuela, and Ecuador under Bolivar's presidency. (from the Colombia chronicle; sources: Gran Colombia)
- 1822-1830: **Union with Gran Colombia** — Liberated Quito joins Simon Bolivar's Republic of Gran Colombia alongside Venezuela and New Granada until the union breaks apart. (from the Ecuador chronicle; sources: Gran Colombia; Simon Bolivar)
- 1830 CE: **Breakup of Gran Colombia** — Venezuela and Ecuador secede and Bolivar dies near Santa Marta; the union dissolves, leaving New Granada as a separate state. (from the Colombia chronicle; sources: Gran Colombia; Simon Bolivar)
- 13 May 1830: **Republic of Ecuador founded** — An assembly in Quito separates the southern district from Gran Colombia and founds the Republic of Ecuador, with Juan Jose Flores as first president. (from the Ecuador chronicle; sources: History of Ecuador; Gran Colombia)
