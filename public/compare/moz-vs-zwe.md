## Entangled histories (3 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 14th–15th century: **Kilwa controls the Sofala gold trade** — The sultanate of Kilwa dominates the export of interior gold through Sofala, drawing wealth from the Zimbabwe and Mutapa goldfields into the wider Islamic world. (from the Mozambique chronicle; sources: Sofala; Sofala)
- c. 15th century: **Rise of the Mutapa empire** — The Mutapa (Mwenemutapa) empire emerged toward the Zambezi, inheriting Great Zimbabwe's gold trade and ruling much of the northern plateau and parts of present-day Mozambique. (from the Zimbabwe chronicle; sources: Zimbabwe: History; Mutapa Empire; Torwa: historical state, Africa)
- c. 1966–1979: **The liberation war (Second Chimurenga)** — Guerrilla forces of ZANU (ZANLA) and ZAPU (ZIPRA), based in Mozambique and Zambia, waged an escalating war against the Rhodesian state through the 1970s. (from the Zimbabwe chronicle; sources: Rhodesian Bush War (Second Chimurenga); Zimbabwe: History; Robert Mugabe)
