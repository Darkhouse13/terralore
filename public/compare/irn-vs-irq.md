## Entangled histories (7 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 539 BCE: **Cyrus the Great captures Babylon** — The Persian king Cyrus the Great takes Babylon, ending the Neo-Babylonian Empire and bringing Mesopotamia into the Achaemenid Persian realm as one of its richest provinces. (from the Iraq chronicle; sources: History of Mesopotamia; Iraq — History)
- c. 636–637 CE: **Battle of al-Qadisiyyah and the Arab conquest** — Arab Muslim armies of the Rashidun Caliphate defeat the Sasanians at al-Qadisiyyah near al-Hira and take Ctesiphon, ending Persian rule in Mesopotamia and beginning the region's Islamisation. (from the Iraq chronicle; sources: Battle of al-Qādisiyyah; Iraq — History)
- 9th century CE: **The House of Wisdom and the Golden Age** — Under Harun al-Rashid and al-Ma'mun, Baghdad's scholars — associated with the Bayt al-Hikmah, the 'House of Wisdom' — translate and advance Greek, Persian and Indian learning during the Islamic Golden Age. (from the Iraq chronicle; sources: Hārūn al-Rashīd — Abbasid caliph; Bayt al-Ḥikmah — House of Wisdom)
- 1638 CE: **Ottoman conquest of Baghdad** — The Ottoman Empire takes definitive control of Baghdad from Safavid Persia, ruling Mesopotamia for nearly three centuries as the provinces of Mosul, Baghdad and Basra. (from the Iraq chronicle; sources: Iraq — History)
- 1980–1988: **The Iran–Iraq War** — Iraq invades in September 1980, igniting a devastating eight-year war; Iran accepts UN Security Council Resolution 598 and a ceasefire takes effect on 20 August 1988. (from the Iran chronicle; sources: Iran–Iraq War; United Nations Security Council Resolution 598)
- 1980–1988: **The Iran–Iraq War** — Iraq invades Iran in September 1980, beginning an eight-year war of attrition that kills an estimated several hundred thousand to over a million people on both sides before a UN-mediated ceasefire takes effect in 1988. (from the Iraq chronicle; sources: Iran–Iraq War)
- 1990–1991: **Invasion of Kuwait and the Persian Gulf War** — Iraq invades and annexes Kuwait in August 1990; a United States-led coalition of many nations expels Iraqi forces in early 1991, after which Iraq is placed under severe international sanctions. (from the Iraq chronicle; sources: Persian Gulf War)
