## Entangled histories (5 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 27 Mar 1918: **Union of Bessarabia with Romania** — The Sfatul Tarii voted to unite Bessarabia with Romania (27 March, Old Style; 9 April, New Style); the union, at first conditional on autonomy, was made unconditional in November 1918. (from the Moldova chronicle; sources: Union of 27 March 1918: the National Archive Agency provides access to the Sfatul Tarii collection; Moldova: History)
- 28 Oct 1920: **International recognition of the union** — A treaty signed in Paris by Romania, Britain, France, Italy and Japan recognized the union of Bessarabia with Romania, though the Soviet Union never accepted it. (from the Moldova chronicle; sources: Union of 27 March 1918: the National Archive Agency provides access to the Sfatul Tarii collection; Moldova: History)
- 28 Jun 1940: **Soviet annexation of Bessarabia** — Acting on the secret protocol of the Molotov-Ribbentrop Pact, the Red Army occupied Bessarabia after an ultimatum to Romania, ending more than two decades of Romanian rule. (from the Moldova chronicle; sources: Bessarabia's Annexation: Impact and Legacy of the USSR Ultimatum; Moldova: History)
- 28 Jun 1940: **Soviet seizure of Bessarabia and northern Bukovina** — The Soviet Union demanded and took Bessarabia and northern Bukovina from Romania, the first of the 1940 territorial losses; Bessarabia, largely Romanian-speaking, became the core of the later Republic of Moldova. (from the Romania chronicle; sources: Romania; Moldova and Romania: A Long and Complicated Relationship)
- 27 Aug 1991: **Moldova's independence and the Romanian bond** — As the Soviet Union dissolved, the former Bessarabia declared independence as the Republic of Moldova, immediately recognised by Romania, with which it shares the Romanian language and a common history. (from the Romania chronicle; sources: Moldova; Moldova and Romania: A Long and Complicated Relationship)
