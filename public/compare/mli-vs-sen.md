## Entangled histories (4 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- c. 1286: **Takrur drawn into Mali's orbit** — Political fragmentation and the rise of the Mali Empire bring Takrur under outside dynasties, ending its independent prominence. (from the Senegal chronicle; sources: Takrur)
- 1959: **Mali Federation formed** — Senegal and the Sudanese Republic unite in the Mali Federation, seeking a federal path out of French rule. (from the Senegal chronicle; sources: Mali Federation; Mali Federation)
- January 1959: **The Mali Federation with Senegal** — French Soudan and Senegal join in the short-lived Mali Federation, with Modibo Keïta as a leading figure; it dissolves in August 1960. (from the Mali chronicle; sources: Mali Federation; Modibo Keita — 1st President of Mali)
- 4 April 1960: **Independence within the Mali Federation** — France grants the Mali Federation independence, transferring power on 4 April 1960; full independence follows on 20 June. (from the Senegal chronicle; sources: Mali Federation; Mali Federation)
