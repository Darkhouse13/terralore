- 1260 CE: **Battle of Ain Jalut** — Mamluk forces defeat the Mongols at Ain Jalut in Palestine, halting their westward advance and securing Egypt as the bastion of the Islamic world. (from the Egypt chronicle; sources: Egypt: The Ayyubid dynasty (1171–1250))
