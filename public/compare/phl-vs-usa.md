## Entangled histories (3 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- 10 Dec 1898: **Treaty of Paris: Spain cedes the Philippines to the U.S.** — By the Treaty of Paris ending the Spanish-American War, Spain cedes the Philippines to the United States without consulting Filipinos, who had already declared independence. (from the Philippines chronicle; sources: The Philippine-American War, 1899-1902 (Treaty of Paris cession))
- 1899-1902: **The Philippine-American War** — Filipino nationalists under Aguinaldo fight the United States for independence; the war kills thousands of combatants and as many as 200,000 civilians before ending in 1902. (from the Philippines chronicle; sources: The Philippine-American War, 1899-1902 (Treaty of Paris cession); Philippine-American War (and the Commonwealth))
- 4 Jul 1946: **Independence of the Republic of the Philippines** — The United States relinquishes sovereignty and recognises Philippine independence under the Treaty of Manila; Manuel Roxas becomes first president of the republic. (from the Philippines chronicle; sources: A Guide to the United States' History of Recognition: The Philippines; July 4, 1946: The Philippines Gained Independence from the United States)
