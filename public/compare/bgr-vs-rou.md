## Entangled histories (4 events)

Events in which either nation's sourced chronicle names the other — including under earlier names of the same state. Each is the record exactly as its own chronicle carries it, with that chronicle's sources.

- Jun–Aug 1913: **Second Balkan War and the loss of Macedonia** — Bulgaria attacked its former allies over the division of Macedonia and was crushed by Serbia, Greece, Romania and the Ottomans; the Treaty of Bucharest left most of Macedonia to its rivals and Southern Dobruja to Romania. (from the Bulgaria chronicle; sources: Second Balkan War; Balkan Wars)
- 30 Aug 1940: **Second Vienna Award and Southern Dobruja** — Under German and Italian arbitration Romania ceded northern Transylvania to Hungary; in September southern Dobruja was ceded to Bulgaria, costing Romania about 30% of its territory and people. (from the Romania chronicle; sources: Romania)
- 7 Sep 1940: **Treaty of Craiova returns Southern Dobruja** — By the Treaty of Craiova, Romania ceded Southern Dobruja back to Bulgaria, restoring the 1912 frontier, the one Axis-era border change that was not reversed after the Second World War. (from the Bulgaria chronicle; sources: Treaty of Craiova; Treaty of Craiova and the return of South Dobruja)
- 1 Jan 2007: **Accession to the European Union** — On 1 January 2007 Romania, with Bulgaria, joined the European Union, raising it to twenty-seven member states and completing Romania's integration into the West. (from the Romania chronicle; sources: Bulgaria and Romania join the European Union)
