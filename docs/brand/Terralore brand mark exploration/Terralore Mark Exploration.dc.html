1a
I · STRATA — "THE FAULT"

Five strata of time, broken by a fault: the layers shift where history does. It compresses the site's ownable timeline motif — eras as layered, datable material — into a silhouette no clean-grid logo can copy. It reads as archive, core sample and timeline at once; the burnt-orange stratum marks the present era, but the mark carries entirely in one color.

1b
II · MERIDIAN — "THE ZENITH"

The globe stripped to its drawing geometry: a horizon, a dome, and meridians converging at the zenith — the wireframe of Terralore's own 3D globe, not a picture of Earth. No landmass, no projection center: politically neutral by construction. The horizon extending past the dome keeps it from closing into the generic corporate circle-globe; the zenith point takes the accent.

1c
III · WORDMARK — "THE IMPRINT"

Identity carried by the word itself, ended with a full stop — the confidence of a reference work that intends to be the last word. The reduction is a printer's-mark monogram: T and L cut as one glyph, crossbar reaching back (terra, the ground), foot reaching forward (lore, the telling). It sits beside mastheads, not app icons.
